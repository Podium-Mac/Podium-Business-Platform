## About Podium
Podium is a comprehensive business communication platform that enables local businesses and service providers to interact with customers through modern messaging channels, manage online reputation, and process payments efficiently. The platform consolidates customer interactions from multiple channels including text messaging, web chat, and social media into a unified inbox, simplifying communication management for businesses that rely on customer relationships. Available for both MacOS and Windows, Podium helps businesses transition from traditional phone-based communication to digital messaging while maintaining personal connections with customers and streamlining operational workflows.

The platform's messaging capabilities are designed specifically for business contexts, featuring automated responses, conversation routing, and team collaboration tools that ensure timely and consistent customer communication. Podium integrates review management functionality that automatically solicits customer feedback after service interactions and provides tools for responding to reviews across major platforms. With built-in payment processing, appointment scheduling, and lead management features, the platform serves as an all-in-one solution for businesses seeking to enhance customer experience, increase online visibility, and simplify financial transactions through digital tools adapted to modern consumer preferences.
